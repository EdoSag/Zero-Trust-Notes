Zero-Trust Notes is a fast, simple notes app for everything you want to remember: ideas, shopping lists, recipes, meeting notes and voice memos. It looks and feels like the notes app you know, except that only you can read what's inside.

WHAT IT DOES
• Notes and checklists: ticked items drop to the bottom, and a note turns into a list and back.
• Grid or list view, pin to top, colours, drag to reorder.
• Notebooks and tags, with search and filters across everything.
• Images, drawings and handwriting, encrypted like the rest.
• Copy text from a photo, read on the phone itself.
• Voice notes with on-device transcription in English and Hebrew. Your voice never leaves the phone.
• Time and place reminders ("remind me when I get to the supermarket"). The phone watches for the place itself.
• Lock single notes behind an extra fingerprint check.
• Share notes with other Zero-Trust users, still end-to-end encrypted.
• Archive, and a trash that keeps deleted notes for 30 days.
• Share into Notes from any app, and home-screen widgets.
• Import from Google Keep (Google Takeout) in one step, and export your notes any time.

WHY ZERO-TRUST
• End-to-end encrypted: everything is encrypted on your phone before it syncs. We can't read it, and neither can anyone who breaks into the server.
• One private account for every Zero-Trust app, unlocked with your fingerprint or face.
• Works offline and syncs across your devices when you're back online.
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no data sold. Ever.
• Open source (AGPL-3.0), so anyone can check what the app does.
• English and Hebrew, with full right-to-left support.

Part of the Zero-Trust suite: Notes, Tasks, Calendar, Contacts, Phone, Fitness, Meds, Time and Browser. One account opens them all.
